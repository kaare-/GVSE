# Voice building-block session

A recording list for one studio day.  
These are not songs, characters, or finished sound effects. They are **raw pieces** we will cut, layer, pitch, and process into everything in a simulation (water, rock, weather, small creatures, and so on).

If a take sounds a bit plain and “too close,” that is correct.

- **Time:** about 4–5 hours including breaks, or two shorter sessions
- **Format:** WAV · 48 kHz · 24-bit · mono preferred
- **Must finish:** sections 1 and 6. If we run out of time, stop after section 6.
- **Please stop** if the voice is tired. We can pick up later sections another day.

You will **not** need lyrics, melody, jazz repertoire, animal or instrument impressions, or acting.

---

## How to sing and speak these

- **No words.** No “water,” no scat as a tune, no humming a song.
- **Long tones: no vibrato.** Straight, like a synth. Musical expression is only for the rise / fall items in section 4.
- **Hold still** on long tones. We need a boring, stable middle we can loop.
- Comfortable voice only. No screaming, no damage. “Pressed” means slightly effortful, not a belt.
- If a sound is unclear, do a spoken example first, then the take.
- Two takes of anything that felt unstable. Label them `_a` and `_b`.

**Pitches — decide with the engineer in two minutes.**  
Pick three notes you can hold easily, about an octave apart. Call them **LOW / MID / HIGH**. Use those all day. Write the note names in the delivery note (for example A2 / A3 / A4).

---

## For the studio / engineer

- Dry room. No reverb, delay, chorus, or “make it nice.”
- One consistent close mic (about 15–20 cm). Same position the whole day.
- Pop filter **on** for long tones and air. Pop filter **off** for the short hits (section 6).
- Leave a little headroom. Do not brickwall.
- Slate each item: speak the **item number and name**, one second of silence, then the sound.
- One WAV per item. If an item is “20 hits,” that is still one file, hits separated by silence.

**File name:** `024_fall_fast_oo.wav`  
(number from the list + short name)

**Delivery:** a folder of WAVs, plus a short text file of anything that was skipped or substituted.

Please do **not** send processed files, songs, or “I also made a water sound.” Extra experiments are welcome only if they are clearly labelled `EXTRA_` and kept separate.

## 4. Moves — rise, fall, arch, “wah”

*About 45 minutes.*

Now you can be a little more like a singer. Still **no tune** and **no words**. One clean gesture per take.

- **Slow** ≈ 3–4 seconds
- **Fast** ≈ half a second, still clear, not sloppy

**Rise** = slide up about an octave.  
**Fall** = slide down about an octave.  
**Arch** = up, then down, same range.

## Please skip rather than substitute

If something is impossible that day, skip it and write it down. A hole we know about is better than a substitute that is actually a different sound.

Please do not send:

- A finished “water sound” or “monster sound”
- Songs, improvisations, or unlabelled extras
- Processed files (no reverb, no pitch plugins on the recordings)
- Takes with words

---

## Why the plain takes matter

We will treat **004–015** as engines, **016–037** as colours, **038–067** as moves, **068–077** as textures, and **078–088** as hits. One later cue might be three of your files stacked and pitched. You do not need to imagine the game. If the take is clean, steady, and labelled, it is useful.

Thank you.

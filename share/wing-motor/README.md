Two existing recordings, cut into gestures by phrase length. These files are here so they can be downloaded. They are not part of the voxel engine.

A phrase shorter than 0.8 seconds is b1 clicks only. A longer phrase also carries the 162 Hz tone from b2. Silences of at least 300 ms stay silent. The left channel is the left wing. The right channel is the right wing. The circuit is the same half-gap model as the other wing-motor renders.

- `motor-halfgap-cut-twinkle.wav` — 126.7 s. Derrick Coetzee, Twinkle Twinkle Little Star, CC0. Every line is long, so every line has the tone. The gaps between lines are quiet.
- `motor-halfgap-cut-breves.wav` — 212.9 s. Magdalen Kadel, Breves dies hominis, public domain. The short notes are clicks only. The held notes bring the tone.

The test note is with the research documents, not in this repository.

One phrase in which the wing muscles change gesture. This file is here so it can be downloaded. It is not part of the voxel engine.

Nine seconds. Three times: one second of pulse clicks (b1 only), a third of a second of silence, one second in which b2 joins, a third of a second of silence. Then one more second of silence. A b1 spike is a 4 ms click at 260 Hz. A b2 spike is 6 ms of 162 Hz. The same +3.5 mV bias as the other wing-motor renders.

`motor-halfgap-bouts.wav` is seed 0: 144 b1 events and 50 b2 events. The gaps are silent.

The test note is with the research documents, not in this repository.

One existing recording, driven only by its loudness envelope. This file is here so it can be downloaded. It is not part of the voxel engine, and this branch does not need to be merged.

No phrase is assigned a pulse train or a steady tone. The left channel is the left wing. The right channel is the right wing. A b1 spike is the 260 Hz click. A b2 spike is 6 ms of 162 Hz. Silences of at least 300 ms stay silent. The circuit is the same half-gap model as the other wing-motor renders.

- `motor-halfgap-envelope-twinkle.wav` — 126.7 s. Derrick Coetzee, Twinkle Twinkle Little Star, CC0. Both muscles are in the lines. The tone is uneven inside them. The gaps between lines are quiet.

The test note is with the research documents, not in this repository.

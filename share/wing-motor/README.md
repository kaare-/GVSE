Six listening files from four scored tries. They are here so they can be downloaded. They are not part of the voxel engine, and this branch does not need to be merged.

No try is a rehearsal patch. The left channel is the left wing. The right channel is the right wing. A b1 spike is the 260 Hz click. A b2 spike is 6 ms of 162 Hz. The drive is the loudness envelope of an existing recording, except where a try adds a labeled decay or a labeled integral on b2.

- `motor-halfgap-thin-twinkle.wav` — 126.7 s. Derrick Coetzee, Twinkle Twinkle Little Star, CC0. Try 1, thinning score. Try 3 listens to this same file.
- `motor-halfgap-thin-breves.wav` — 212.9 s. Magdalen Kadel, Breves dies hominis, public domain. Try 1.
- `motor-halfgap-adapt-twinkle.wav` and `motor-halfgap-adapt-breves.wav` — try 2, a 1 mV decay on b2.
- `motor-halfgap-build-twinkle.wav` and `motor-halfgap-build-breves.wav` — try 4, a 1 second integral on b2, ceiling 2 mV.

The test note is with the research documents, not in this repository.

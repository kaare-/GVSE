Listening renders in which b1 and b2 drive the sound. These files are here so they can be downloaded. They are not part of the voxel engine.

A b1 spike is a 4 ms click at 260 Hz. A b2 spike is 6 ms of 162 Hz. The cells sit 3.5 mV closer to threshold than the published model. Without that bias the wing muscles stay silent. Silence in the first file is the check that the bias does not sing by itself.

- `motor-halfgap-silence.wav` — 6 s, no spikes.
- `motor-halfgap-pulse.wav` — 6 s. Pulse drive. b1 only, 64 clicks.
- `motor-halfgap-steady.wav` — 6 s. Steady drive. b1 rushes and b2 joins.
- `motor-halfgap-twinkle.wav` — 126.7 s. Derrick Coetzee, Twinkle Twinkle Little Star, CC0, through the same map. 1,943 b1 events and 307 b2 events. The rests are silent.

The test note is with the research documents, not in this repository.
